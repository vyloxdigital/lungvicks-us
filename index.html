The Herbal Formula

Key Botanicals Inside Every
LungVicks Capsule

LungVicks combines time-honored respiratory herbs with modern nutritional science — each ingredient chosen for its documented contribution to airway comfort, lung tissue protection, or mucus support.

Ingredient 01
Mullein Leaf Extract
Traditional Lung Herb

Mullein (Verbascum thapsus) is one of the most historically significant respiratory herbs in North American and European folk medicine traditions. Its leaf extract contains saponins and mucilage compounds that help soothe inflamed bronchial membranes and support the expulsion of excess mucus from airways.

Herbalists have used mullein specifically for the sensation of “tired, heavy lungs” — making it a cornerstone of the LungVicks formula’s cool relief approach. See traditional uses documented at NIH National Library of Medicine.

Ingredient 02
Peppermint Leaf Extract
Cool Airway Comfort

Peppermint’s menthol-containing compounds are the source of the characteristic “cool” sensation in LungVicks. Menthol activates cold-sensitive receptors in the airway mucosa, creating a refreshing perception of open, clear breathing. It also has mild bronchodilatory and antispasmodic effects on airway smooth muscle.

The NIH Office of Dietary Supplements acknowledges peppermint’s menthol content and its documented effects on respiratory comfort and sinus relief.

Ingredient 03
Licorice Root Extract
Soothing Expectorant

Licorice root (Glycyrrhiza glabra) has a 4,000-year documented history as a respiratory herb across Chinese, Ayurvedic, and Mediterranean healing traditions. Its glycyrrhizin and flavonoid compounds have demonstrated anti-inflammatory, expectorant, and broncho-soothing properties that help calm irritated airways and thin secretions.

Research indexed at NIH PubMed has examined licorice root compounds for anti-inflammatory activity in respiratory tissue, particularly in relation to airway irritation and mucus management.

Ingredient 04
Thyme Herb Extract
Antimicrobial Respiratory Herb

Thyme (Thymus vulgaris) contains thymol and carvacrol — volatile compounds with demonstrated antimicrobial, antispasmodic, and expectorant properties in airway tissue. Thyme preparations have been used in European herbal medicine for centuries as a primary herb for bronchial comfort and productive cough relief.

Clinical evidence indexed at NIH NLM supports thyme’s antispasmodic and mucus-clearing properties, making it particularly well-suited to LungVicks’ bedtime respiratory formula.

Ingredient 05
N-Acetyl Cysteine (NAC)
Mucolytic Antioxidant

NAC is a precursor to glutathione — the body’s most potent internal antioxidant. In lung tissue, NAC plays a dual role: it directly breaks the disulfide bonds that make mucus thick and viscous, and it replenishes glutathione stores that protect bronchial cells from oxidative damage caused by environmental pollutants.

Extensive research on NIH PubMed establishes NAC as one of the most evidence-supported mucolytic compounds in respiratory supplement science, with decades of clinical study behind its airway applications.

Ingredient 06
Vitamin C & Zinc
Immune & Structural Support

Vitamin C is essential for collagen synthesis — maintaining the structural integrity of bronchial walls and alveolar membranes. As a water-soluble antioxidant in airway fluid, it neutralizes inhaled oxidants before they damage tissue. Zinc supports immune defense at airway mucosa surfaces, the body’s first line of protection against airborne pathogens.

The NIH ODS Vitamin C fact sheet and FDA dietary supplement guidance both confirm the roles of Vitamin C and Zinc in supporting immune function and respiratory tissue health.
